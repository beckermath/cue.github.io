A place where anyone can feel at home

The Good Day Cafe was created to be a place that makes people comfortable on their first visit, and a place they’ll make their own. It welcomes guests with warmth and hospitality – to meet, have a cup of coffee, share a great meal, and hang out. Details add to the restaurant’s charm, including rough-hewn wood beams, warm colors, and custom designed murals, floors, metalwork, and lighting. Menus use naive, colorful illustrations to make the place feel friendly and approachable; a place where anyone can feel at home.
